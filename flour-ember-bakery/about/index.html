Who We Are

Flour & Ember Bakery is an artisan bakery in Birmingham — built on a simple principle: do the job well, communicate clearly, and never leave a client guessing.

We work with clients in Birmingham, providing Sourdough Bread, Custom Cakes, Pastries. Our team brings genuine expertise to every job, but what our clients most consistently mention is how straightforward it is to work with us: honest advice, realistic timelines, and no hidden costs.

What sets Flour & Ember Bakery apart is quality work and honest communication. We're not the biggest artisan bakery in the area, but we're proud to be the one that clients refer to their friends — and that says more about our work than any marketing could.
